Each partner should complete a form like this one and provide a copy to each of their partners as part of their Poly Relationship Agreement. You can choose to fill this out only once and provide each partner with an identical copy or fill out a unique version for each partner individually. 
 
The partner receiving this document must respect the boundaries ("no" responses) defined herein or else the stability of the relationship may come into question. 
 
It is not necessary for both partners in a relationship to give the same answers. Consequently, you could have a situation where you are required to reveal all of the details of your dates but you prefer that your partner not disclose anything about their dates to you. Alternatively, you may have one partner who wants to know details and another who prefers to know nothing. In the event that your partners have different or even conflicting expectations of you, you can either opt to accept the most restrictive set of expectations or try to negotiate for leniency with the more restrictive partner.
 
This should be considered a living document. Therefore, it is recommended that it expire at least annually so that you can capture any ways in which your relationship has evolved and ensure that your partner is working from the most accurate version. Note that you can update or change this document at any time. Of course, it is your responsibility to notify your partners if/when you do.
 
When replying "Maybe/Sometimes" be sure to include wat factors would sway you toward a yes or a no in general.
NTH : "Nice To Have" : A thing that you would like to have happen but isn't strictly required to happen.
