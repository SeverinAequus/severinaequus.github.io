What's the matter with these kids these days?
Thursday, September 22, 2022
8:20 AM
 
One major factor driving the breakdown of society is the economy. Specifically, the fact that the USA is in the latest stage of capitalism. One could even argue that our economy was supposed to have collapsed in 2008 and now we’re in some undefined stage of capitalism which I will herein name “Zombie-stage Capitalism”.
 
In ZsC, none of the old wisdom applies anymore. Everything is a funhouse mirror reflection of what it used to be; companies, jobs, markets, all of it. The young people grow up under no delusions about the precarious nature of the world they live in, understanding full well the burden that the sins of the father place upon their shoulders.
 
Consequently, they don’t have the same values. The older generation perceives this as a degeneration. But it is in fact a result of a rapidly changing environment that the younger people are doing their best to adapt and survive in.
 
And that is the objective decline. Standard of living. The measurable, objective decline from living to merely surviving. We have done our children a grave injustice!
