A Third Party Vote is a Vote for the Boogey Man
2016
Severin Aequus
 
"A vote for a third party is a vote for Trump"

This tired old trope is unleashed from the propaganda pit Every. Single. Election. Cycle. It did get a bit of a reprieve in 2012 because Obama's reelection was a given. So I guess that's why people think they're being insightful this time.

For one thing, you do realize there are also people saying, "A vote for a third party is a vote for Hillary", right? It's not logic, it's a logical fallacy called the False Dichotomy dressed up in populist attire.

On the surface, it's a convincing argument. That's why it gets used so often. In order to unravel it, we have to look at the essence of our obligation as members of society and our ethical obligations to ourselves and others.

You are not responsible for how other people vote.

Read that sentence until you truly believe it, because it's true. That logical fallacy rests on your willingness to predict how other people will act and take responsibility for their actions. 

Doing that is a sure fire way to end up with a prescription for Xanax.

You are only responsible for yourself. Your vote is your explicit endorsement for another human being to represent your interests. Period. If you were coerced by some faulty logic into giving your explicit endorsement for a person you do not want to represent you, then you are a fool. Plain and simple.
